System Architecture

The monitoring rig continuously samples key physical properties from the hydraulic system:

  • Differential Pressure Transducers (4–20 mA): Installed across the inline high-pressure filter element to track pressure drop ($\Delta P$).
  • Oil Temperature & Reservoir Level (PT100 / Float Switch): Monitored to prevent thermal breakdown of hydraulic fluid and detect tank leakage.
  • Vibration Sensor (Piezoelectric Accelerometer): Mounted on the pump housing to monitor axial and radial vibration profiles.

Data Acquisition & Logic

Signals are captured via RS485 Modbus RTU and processed by an ESP32 microcontroller acting as an edge node:

  • Filter Clogging Alert: Triggers an early warning when differential pressure exceeds $2.5\text{ bar}$, signaling required maintenance before the bypass valve opens.
  • Thermal Interlock: Sends a digital trip signal to the main pump contactor if oil temperature passes $65^\circ\text{C}$.
  • Telemetry: Published via MQTT over Ethernet/Wi-Fi to an on-premise Grafana engine for live trend visualization and logging.
